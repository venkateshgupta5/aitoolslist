# aitoolslist.ai — Backlink Outreach Templates

Copy/paste and personalize. Keep every email short, specific, and genuinely useful —
never buy links or use link-exchange schemes (Google penalizes them and it can undo the
indexing work already done). The order below is by ROI.

---

## 1. Featured-company badge outreach  ⭐ highest ROI

Send to every company whose tool you've profiled. They have a real incentive to link back
("As featured on…") and it compounds every edition. The badge + embed code lives at
**https://aitoolslist.ai/badge**.

**Subject:** We featured {Tool} in our latest AI edition

> Hi {FirstName},
>
> I run aitoolslist.ai, an independent fortnightly publication that researches and
> fact-checks standout AI products. We selected **{Tool}** as our pick in
> *{Category}* this edition — here's the profile: {profile URL}.
>
> It's independent editorial coverage (not a paid placement), and you're welcome to
> show it off: we made a free **"Featured on aitoolslist.ai"** badge you can drop on your
> site — grab the one-line embed here: https://aitoolslist.ai/badge
>
> Either way, if anything in the profile needs correcting, just reply and I'll update it.
>
> Best,
> Venkatesh
> aitoolslist.ai

*Follow-up (after ~5 business days, if no reply):*
> Quick nudge on the above — happy to send the badge snippet directly if easier. No worries
> if now's not the time.

## 2. Newsletter / journalist pitch (digital PR)

Pitch each edition's single most interesting finding to AI newsletters (Ben's Bites, TLDR AI,
The Rundown, Superhuman) and relevant journalists. Lead with the *insight*, not your site.

**Subject:** Data point for {Newsletter}: {one-line finding}

> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Love {Newsletter}. One finding from our latest research you might find useful for readers:
>
> **{One concrete, dated, specific finding — e.g. "As of Nov 2026, the standout open-source
> LLM-observability tool by adoption is X; here's how it compares to Y and Z."}**
>
> Full methodology + the comparison table: {edition or category URL}. Happy to share the
> underlying sources or a quote if helpful.
>
> Thanks,
> Venkatesh — aitoolslist.ai

## 3. HARO / Connectively / Featured.com / Qwoted response

Sign up as a source; answer queries about AI tools. Your research positioning makes you a
natural expert quote → backlinks from real publications.

> I'm Venkatesh, founder of aitoolslist.ai, an independent fortnightly AI-product
> intelligence publication (we research and fact-check AI tools across 14 categories).
>
> {Direct, specific answer to the journalist's question — 2–4 sentences, with a concrete
> example or number. No fluff.}
>
> Happy to expand or share sources. Profile for attribution: https://aitoolslist.ai/about

## 4. Resource-page link request

Search for `"best AI tool directories"`, `"AI tools" + "resources"`, `"AI directory" + "list"`.
Email the page owner.

**Subject:** Addition for your "{Page title}" list

> Hi {FirstName},
>
> Your roundup "{Page title}" ({page URL}) is a great resource. If you're still updating it,
> aitoolslist.ai might fit — it's an independent, fact-checked AI-product publication
> (fortnightly editions, 14 categories, verified profiles with sources).
>
> No worries if it's not a match. Thanks for keeping the list current.
>
> Venkatesh

---

## 5. Unlinked-mention reclamation

When someone names "aitoolslist" without linking (find these in Google/Bing alerts), ask:

> Hi {FirstName} — thanks for the mention of aitoolslist.ai in {URL}! Would you mind linking
> it to https://aitoolslist.ai so readers can find us? Much appreciated.

---

### Hygiene checklist
- Personalize the first line every time (reference their actual tool/article).
- One ask per email. Short. No attachments.
- Track sends in a simple sheet (company, URL, date, reply, link live?).
- Never pay for links, join PBNs, or do bulk "1000 backlinks" gigs.
